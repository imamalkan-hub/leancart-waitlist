## Long description
LeanCart Out-of-Stock Waitlist solves one daily problem for WooCommerce store owners: "being unable to notify customers when stock returns".
Instead of hundreds of options and bloated code, it is a focused tool that works
right after installation.

### Why buy it?
- **Saves time:** Recovers lost sales with buyers ready to purchase when stock returns
- **Minutes to set up:** Works with sensible defaults.
- **Secure:** WordPress CRUD API, nonce and capability checks, proper escaping.
- **Compatible:** WooCommerce 8+, HPOS, PHP 8.0+, WordPress 6.2+.
- **Supported:** 12 months of updates and support, 30-day money-back guarantee.

### Features
* Waitlist form on the product page
* List of signups in the admin panel
* Automatic restock notification
* Captures product, variation, and stock-status changes
* Notified customers can sign up again if the product sells out again

### Who is it for?
Boutiques and stockist stores that struggle with inventory
